# Product — FIRE Calculator India

## What This App Does
A native iOS calculator that tells Indian users:
1. How much corpus (savings) they need to retire early — their "FIRE number"
2. How much to invest monthly (SIP) to reach that number
3. Where to invest it (asset allocation + India-specific vehicles)

## Three FIRE Variants
- **Lean FIRE** — minimal lifestyle, ×20 corpus, 5% withdrawal rate
- **FIRE** — comfortable lifestyle, ×25 corpus, 4% withdrawal rate (primary)
- **Fat FIRE** — luxury lifestyle, ×33 corpus, 3% withdrawal rate

## Three Investment Styles
- **Conservative** — 40% equity / 45% debt / 10% gold / 5% emergency, 9% return
- **Balanced** — 60% equity / 25% debt / 10% gold / 5% emergency, 12% return
- **Aggressive** — 80% equity / 10% debt / 5% gold / 5% emergency, 14% return

## India-Specific Defaults (Do Not Change Without Updating SPEC.md)

| Inflation | 6% | RBI long-run target |

## What This App Is NOT
- Not a portfolio tracker
- Not connected to any backend or broker
- Not a tax filing tool
- Not a mutual fund marketplace
